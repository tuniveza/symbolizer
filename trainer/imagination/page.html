Your design, as the model runs it

Parts

  • Neuron impact: a line symbol that is always oscillating. Alone, a neuron oscillates with itself.
  • Line of neuron impact: the lines between neurons, carrying oscillating impacts.
  • Imagination layer: joined to every neuron. It can redesign any line, whether or not neurons are in sync.

Rules

  • An impact relays only between neurons in sync enough. Otherwise a neuron stays self-contained.
  • What a neuron absorbs becomes its temperature.
  • Heat flows hot to cold, never the other way.
  • Hot neurons exchange. Cold neurons refine their own abstraction.

The cycle every neuron goes round

absorb→heat up→exchange→cool→refine→absorb

In the model, splitting a word into two meanings (nuclear fission) only happens while the neuron is cold, as part of refining itself.

The archetypes, compared

Each archetype now drives the network. Its personality sets the 16 energies (its 4 functions lead, the other 4 are weak) and steers heat and order while it runs. Its decisions set the rules: how strict a meaning must be, how soon a word splits, how long unused lines last, how soon hidden neurons join, and how much practice it replays. All 48 were trained on the same word test, 6 runs each.

What it showed

I ran the model many times with different random starts and measured it. "Right" means the word had split, and the sense that won in a context was the correct meaning.
